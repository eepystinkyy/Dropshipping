# 04 — 90-Day Execution Plan

Four phases with **kill gates** between them. The gates are the point: they convert "I
have a feeling this isn't working" into a decision you made in advance, when you were
calm and had no money on the table.

Budget: **$2,000–5,000**. Note how little of it is ads.

| Category | Allocation |
|---|---|
| Samples (3 candidates × 2–3 units) | $300–600 |
| Content production (lighting, tripod, props) | $200–400 |
| Store + apps, 3 months | $150–300 |
| Entity + legal setup | $200–500 |
| First inventory buy (phase 4) | $1,000–2,500 |
| Paid ads | $150–700 **only after Gate 3** |

---

## Phase 1 — Research (Weeks 1–3)

No money spent except samples.

- [ ] Write down 3–5 audiences you have genuine proximity to
- [ ] Pick the strongest, list 10 candidate products in it
- [ ] Apply the 10 hard filters in [`02-product-selection.md`](02-product-selection.md) → narrow to 3
- [ ] Get 3 supplier quotes each, **DDP, with HTS code and duty rate confirmed**
- [ ] Run `tools/unit_economics.py` on all 3
- [ ] Teardown of top 3 competitors' bad reviews per candidate
- [ ] Order samples of the top 2
- [ ] Verify organic reach is alive in the niche (sub-50k accounts hitting 100k+ views)

### 🚦 Gate 1 — end of week 3
Proceed only if:
- At least one candidate passes **all six hard gates** in `01-unit-economics.md`
- The sample is genuinely good in hand — not "acceptable"
- You can list 20 content ideas for it without straining

**If no candidate passes: go back to Phase 1 with a different audience.** Do not proceed
with the best of a bad set. This is the single most common failure point, and the cost of
looping here is a few hundred dollars versus a few thousand later.

---

## Phase 2 — Build & Content Engine (Weeks 4–6)

- [ ] Register entity, business bank account
- [ ] Shopify store: default theme, 1 product page, bundles configured for $60+ AOV
- [ ] Own photography and video of the actual sample — no supplier stock images
- [ ] Product page: problem-led copy, answering the top 5 objections from competitor reviews
- [ ] Policies, tax settings, payment processing live
- [ ] Klaviyo: welcome flow, abandoned cart, post-purchase
- [ ] TikTok Shop seller account
- [ ] **Film and post 30 videos.** Start before the store is perfect.

The content engine is the deliverable of this phase, not the store. A perfect store with
no traffic is worth exactly zero.

### 🚦 Gate 2 — end of week 6
- Store live, test order placed and fulfilled end-to-end by you
- 30 videos posted
- At least one video over 10k views

**No 10k video in 30 attempts means the content angle is wrong.** Change the angle, not
the effort level. Post 30 more before concluding anything about the product.

---

## Phase 3 — Organic Validation (Weeks 7–10)

The critical phase. Goal: **first 50 orders at near-zero CAC.**

- [ ] Post daily. Non-negotiable. 1–3 videos/day.
- [ ] Seed 5–10 micro-creators with free product (10–20% affiliate commission)
- [ ] Handle every CS ticket personally; log every objection
- [ ] Weekly: iterate the product page against the objection log
- [ ] Track per-order contribution against the model — real numbers, not projections
- [ ] Survey buyers: how they found you, what nearly stopped them

### 🚦 Gate 3 — end of week 10
Proceed to paid only if:
- **≥50 orders**
- Actual contribution margin within 20% of model
- Refund rate **≤6%**
- ≥15% of buyers say they'd buy again / repeat orders appearing
- At least 3 videos over 50k views (repeatable, not one lucky hit)

**If under 20 orders: kill it.** Return to Phase 1. You will have spent ~$1,000 and
learned the process — that is a good outcome, not a failure.

**If 20–50 orders: extend 4 weeks.** Don't add ad spend to an unvalidated product; that
converts a small loss into a large one.

---

## Phase 4 — Scale (Weeks 11–13+)

Only with Gate 3 passed.

- [ ] Bulk order to 3PL — start with 30–60 days of cover, not more
- [ ] Re-run unit economics on held-inventory costs (Scenario C)
- [ ] Introduce paid: **$20/day**, retargeting and lookalikes from buyer data first
- [ ] Scale spend only while LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 — check weekly, cut immediately if it drops
- [ ] Email/SMS flows carrying repeat purchase
- [ ] Second SKU only once the first is stable

### 🚦 Gate 4 — ongoing
- LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1
- Positive contribution after ad spend
- Cash conversion cycle not going negative — **growth consumes cash, and profitable
  businesses die of cash-flow more often than of bad margins**

---

## Weekly cadence

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mon | Numbers review — orders, CM, CAC, refund rate |
| Tue–Fri | Content production and posting |
| Wed | Supplier/ops check |
| Sat | Customer service sweep, objection log |
| Sun | Off. Genuinely. Burnout is the top cause of failure here. |

## The one metric

If you track nothing else: **contribution dollars per order, measured, not projected.**
Revenue is vanity, gross margin percentage is vanity. Contribution dollars are what buy
the next customer.
